    Process

    How each lot is handled

    Wholesale volumes, handled with the care of a family estate, from the grower to the container door.

    01

    Estate-direct sourcing

    Bought from growers we know, so every lot is traceable to its estate and harvest.

    02

    Cleaning & grading

    Destoned, sieved and sorted by size, density and colour to the specification on your contract.

    03

    Quality testing

    Moisture, colour and purity checked before packing. A reference sample is kept for every lot.

    04

    Export handling

    Container stuffing and sealing at origin, export documents and shipment tracking.
